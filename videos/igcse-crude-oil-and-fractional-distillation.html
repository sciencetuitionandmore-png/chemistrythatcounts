Crude oil and fractional distillation

International GCSE4CH13:42 min

Crude oil is a mixture of hydrocarbons (mostly alkanes). Fractional distillation separates it into fractions with similar boiling points.

This video is part of a free series of four-minute revision summaries for Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Chemistry (4CH1). Subtitles are on, and it finishes with an exam tip on the most common mistakes, the key points to learn, and a free one-page cheat sheet.
