Temperature changes

Core practical 3.8

Aim: to investigate the temperature changes when salts dissolve in water, and in neutralisation, displacement and combustion reactions.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide solution are irritants.
  • Copper(II) sulfate solution is harmful. Zinc powder is flammable.
  • Ethanol is highly flammable. Keep the bottle away from flames. Put the cap on the spirit burner to put it out.
  • The copper can gets hot. Let it cool before you touch it.

Part 1Do the experiment

There are four stations. Do each one. The digital thermometer gives your readings: copy them into Part 2.

Method

    Part 2Record your results

    Use your readings. ΔT = final temperature − start temperature (it is negative if the temperature falls). Then work out the heat energy change with Q = m × c × ΔT, where c = 4.2 J/g/°C and 1 cm³ of solution has a mass of 1 g. Write Q as a positive number.
