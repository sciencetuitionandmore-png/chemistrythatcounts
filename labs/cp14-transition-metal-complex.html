The preparation of a transition metal complex

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 14 · Unit 6

Aim: To prepare crystals of tetraamminecopper(II) sulfate monohydrate, [Cu(NH3)4]SO4·H2O, from copper(II) sulfate and calculate the percentage yield.

Safety
  • Concentrated ammonia is corrosive and gives off toxic, pungent ammonia gas that irritates the eyes and lungs. Use it only in a fume cupboard. Wear goggles and gloves.
  • Copper(II) sulfate is harmful if swallowed and an eye irritant. It is toxic to aquatic life: do not pour the filtrate down the sink in large amounts.
  • Ethanol is highly flammable. Keep it away from naked flames. Only warm the copper sulfate solution gently, before any ethanol is used.
  • Wash hands after handling the product. Do not taste or touch it.
[Cu(H2O)6]2+(aq) + 2NH3(aq) → [Cu(H2O)4(OH)2](s) + 2NH4+(aq)  pale blue precipitate
[Cu(H2O)4(OH)2](s) + 4NH3(aq) → [Cu(NH3)4(H2O)2]2+(aq) + 2OH−(aq) + 2H2O(l)  deep blue solution
