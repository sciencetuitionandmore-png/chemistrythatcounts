The oxidation of propan-1-ol to produce propanal and propanoic acid

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 7 · Unit 3

Aim: to oxidise propan-1-ol with acidified potassium dichromate(VI), making propanal by distillation and propanoic acid by heating under reflux, then to test both products.

Safety
  • Potassium dichromate(VI) is toxic, a carcinogen and a skin sensitiser. Wear gloves and eye protection. Avoid skin contact.
  • Propan-1-ol and propanal are highly flammable. Use an electric heating mantle or water bath, not a Bunsen flame.
  • Dilute sulfuric acid is an irritant. Wash splashes off skin at once.
  • Never heat a closed system. The top of a reflux condenser must stay open.
