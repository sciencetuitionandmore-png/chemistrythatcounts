Percentage of oxygen in air

Core practical 2.14

Aim: To find the approximate percentage by volume of oxygen in air, using a metal (iron or copper) that reacts with oxygen.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection.
  • Wear gloves to handle iron wool. It can give you splinters.
  • Copper method: the silica tube and the Bunsen get very hot. Let them cool before you touch them.
  • Clamp the tube so it cannot fall. Wipe up spilled water.
