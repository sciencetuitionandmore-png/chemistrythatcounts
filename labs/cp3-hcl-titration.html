Finding the concentration of hydrochloric acid

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 3 · Unit 3

Aim: To dilute a solution of hydrochloric acid accurately and then titrate it against standard sodium hydroxide solution, to find the concentration of the original acid.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Sodium hydroxide solution, even at 0.100 mol dm⁻³, is an irritant and can damage the eyes. Wash splashes with plenty of water.
  • The undiluted hydrochloric acid (about 2 mol dm⁻³) is an irritant. Handle it carefully.
  • Always use a pipette filler. Never pipette by mouth.
  • Fill the burette below eye level, with the burette removed from the clamp or the clamp low, using a funnel.
Key fact: HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H2O(l). The acid and alkali react in a 1 : 1 ratio.
