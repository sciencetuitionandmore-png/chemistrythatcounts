Paper chromatography of inks

Core practical 1.13

Aim: to separate the dyes in inks by paper chromatography and use Rf values to identify them.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection.
  • Wipe up spilled water at once so nobody slips.
  • Some inks and food colourings stain skin and clothes. Handle the spots carefully.
  • If you use a solvent other than water (for example ethanol), keep it away from flames.

    Rf = distance moved by the spot ÷ distance moved by the solvent. Give Rf to 2 decimal places.
