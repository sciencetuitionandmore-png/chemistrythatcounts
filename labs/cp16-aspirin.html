The preparation of aspirin

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 16 · Unit 6

Aim: To prepare aspirin from 2-hydroxybenzoic acid, purify it by recrystallisation, and test its purity by melting point.

Safety
  • Ethanoic anhydride is corrosive, flammable and its vapour is very irritating to the eyes and lungs. Use it in a fume cupboard. Wear goggles and gloves.
  • Concentrated phosphoric(V) acid (or sulfuric acid) is corrosive. Add only a few drops with a dropper.
  • Ethanol is highly flammable (bp 78 °C). Heat it only in a hot water bath. No naked flames anywhere near it.
  • 2-Hydroxybenzoic acid and aspirin are harmful if swallowed and irritate the eyes. Never taste your product.
HOOCC6H4OH + (CH3CO)2O → HOOCC6H4OCOCH3 + CH3COOH
2-hydroxybenzoic acid (M = 138) + ethanoic anhydride (M = 102) → aspirin (M = 180) + ethanoic acid (M = 60)
