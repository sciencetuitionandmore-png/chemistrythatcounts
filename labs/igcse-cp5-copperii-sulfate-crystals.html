Making copper(II) sulfate crystals

Core practical 2.42

Aim: to prepare a sample of pure, dry hydrated copper(II) sulfate crystals, starting from copper(II) oxide.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Dilute sulfuric acid is an irritant.
  • Copper(II) sulfate is harmful. Do not touch the crystals with your fingers.
  • Heat gently. Hot acid and hot evaporating basins can burn. Let them cool before you touch them.
  • Never heat to dryness: hot solution can spit.
