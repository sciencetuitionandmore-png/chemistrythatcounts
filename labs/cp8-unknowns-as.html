Analysis of some inorganic and organic unknowns

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 8 · Unit 3

Aim: to identify four inorganic salts and three organic liquids, using test-tube tests for ions and functional groups.

Safety
  • Concentrated hydrochloric acid and phosphorus(V) chloride are corrosive. PCl5 reacts violently with water and gives HCl fumes: use it in a fume cupboard, with gloves and eye protection.
  • Concentrated ammonia gives an irritant gas. Use small volumes in a fume cupboard.
  • Potassium dichromate(VI) is toxic and a carcinogen. Wear gloves.
  • The organic liquids are flammable and 1-bromobutane is harmful. Warm them in a water bath, never over a flame.

Flame test

Clean the nichrome wire, then drag the flame test wire onto an inorganic tube.
Detective tip: test for carbonate first, with dilute acid.

Method

  1. Flame test each inorganic unknown (clean wire each time)
  2. Add dilute acid to test for carbonate
  3. Halide: dilute HNO3, then AgNO3(aq)
  4. Add dilute then conc NH3 to any silver halide precipitate
  5. Sulfate: dilute HCl, then BaCl2(aq)
  6. Ammonium: warm with NaOH(aq), test gas with damp red litmus
  7. Bromine water on each organic unknown
  8. PCl5 on each organic unknown (fume cupboard)
  9. Warm with acidified K2Cr2O7
  10. Warm with ethanolic AgNO3
  11. Add NaHCO3(aq)
