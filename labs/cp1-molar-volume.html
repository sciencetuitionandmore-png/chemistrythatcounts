Measuring the molar volume of a gas

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 1 · Unit 3

Aim: To find the volume occupied by one mole of carbon dioxide at room temperature and pressure, by reacting known masses of calcium carbonate with excess ethanoic acid.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Ethanoic acid at 1.0 mol dm⁻³ is low hazard but can irritate the eyes and skin. Wash splashes off with water.
  • Calcium carbonate is low hazard. Avoid making dust.
  • Push the bung in gently with a twisting action and hold the boiling tube near the top, so the glass does not break.
  • Wipe up spilt water from the trough at once so nobody slips.
Key fact: one mole of any gas occupies about 24.0 dm³ at room temperature and pressure.
