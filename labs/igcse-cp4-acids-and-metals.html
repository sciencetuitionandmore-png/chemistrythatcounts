Acids and metals

Core practical 2.21

Aim: To investigate the reactions between dilute hydrochloric and sulfuric acids and metals (magnesium, zinc, iron and copper).

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Dilute acids are irritants: wash any splashes off your skin with water.
  • Hydrogen is flammable. Only test small amounts, and keep other flames away.
  • The tube with magnesium gets warm. Hold it near the top.
