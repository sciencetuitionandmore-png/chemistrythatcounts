Rates by titration and by an iodine clock

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 9 · Unit 6

Aim: to follow the rate of the iodine–propanone reaction by titration, and to find the order with respect to iodide ions using an iodine clock reaction.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Sulfuric acid (0.5 mol dm−3) is an irritant to eyes and skin.
  • Propanone is highly flammable and irritates the eyes. Keep away from flames.
  • Potassium peroxydisulfate is an oxidiser and may cause skin and breathing sensitisation. Avoid skin contact; do not breathe the dust.
  • Iodine solution stains skin and clothing. Always use a pipette filler, never your mouth.
