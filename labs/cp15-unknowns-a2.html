Analysis of some inorganic and organic unknowns

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 15 · Unit 6

Aim: to identify three transition metal ions and two carbonyl-group organic compounds, using test-tube reactions and spectra.

Safety
  • Sodium hydroxide solution is corrosive to eyes. Wear eye protection.
  • Concentrated hydrochloric acid and concentrated ammonia are corrosive and give irritant fumes. Use a fume cupboard.
  • 2,4-DNPH (Brady's reagent) is toxic and flammable. Wear gloves. Cobalt(II) and nickel(II) salts are carcinogens and skin sensitisers.
  • Make Tollens' reagent fresh. Wash it away at once after use: on standing it can form explosive silver compounds.

Method

  1. Note the colour of each metal ion solution
  2. Add NaOH(aq) dropwise, then in excess, to each
  3. Add NH3(aq) dropwise, then in excess, to each
  4. Add conc HCl to a fresh sample of each
  5. 2,4-DNPH on each organic unknown
  6. Tollens' and Fehling's on each organic unknown
  7. Iodoform test (I2 + NaOH) on each
  8. Na2CO3(aq) on each
  9. Look at the IR and NMR data for each
