Finding the Ka value for a weak acid

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 11 · Unit 6

Aim: to find the acid dissociation constant, Ka, of ethanoic acid by measuring the pH when the acid is exactly half-neutralised.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Sodium hydroxide (0.1 mol dm−3) is an irritant and can damage eyes.
  • Ethanoic acid (0.1 mol dm−3) is low hazard at this concentration, but avoid contact with eyes.
  • Phenolphthalein indicator is dissolved in ethanol, which is highly flammable.
  • The glass bulb of the pH electrode is fragile. Handle it carefully and keep it in its storage solution.
