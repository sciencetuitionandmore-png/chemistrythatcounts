Chlorination of 2-methylpropan-2-ol with concentrated hydrochloric acid

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 6 · Unit 3

Aim: To make 2-chloro-2-methylpropane from 2-methylpropan-2-ol, purify it by washing, drying and distillation, and find the percentage yield.

Safety
  • Concentrated hydrochloric acid is corrosive and gives off an irritating gas. Use it in a fume cupboard. Wear eye protection and gloves.
  • 2-methylpropan-2-ol and 2-chloro-2-methylpropane are highly flammable. No naked flames: heat with an electric heater or a water bath.
  • Pressure builds up in the separating funnel. Release it often by opening the tap, with the funnel upside down and pointing away from people.

(CH3)3COH(l) + HCl(aq) → (CH3)3CCl(l) + H2O(l)
