Finding the formula of a metal oxide

Core practical 1.36

Aim: to find the formula of a metal oxide by burning magnesium in air (combustion) or by reducing copper(II) oxide.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Do not look straight at burning magnesium: the white light can hurt your eyes.
  • The crucible and pipeclay triangle get very hot. Use tongs and let them cool on a heatproof mat.
  • Methane (natural gas) is flammable. Burn off the excess gas at the hole in the tube.
  • Tie back long hair and keep the Bunsen burner away from the edge of the bench.
