Making a standard solution and using it to find the concentration of sodium hydroxide

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 4 · Unit 3

Aim: To make a standard solution of sulfamic acid, H2NSO3H, and use it in a titration to find the concentration of a sodium hydroxide solution.

Safety
  • Sulfamic acid is an irritant to eyes and skin. Wear eye protection. Wash off any spills with plenty of water.
  • Sodium hydroxide solution (about 0.1 mol dm−3) is an irritant and can damage eyes. Wear eye protection throughout.
  • Always use a pipette filler. Never pipette by mouth.
  • Fill the burette below eye level, with a funnel, and remove the funnel before titrating.
