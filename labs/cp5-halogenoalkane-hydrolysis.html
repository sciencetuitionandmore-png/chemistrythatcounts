Investigating the rates of hydrolysis of halogenoalkanes

IAL Chemistry · Core Practical 5 · Unit 3

Aim: To compare how fast different halogenoalkanes are hydrolysed by water, by timing how long a silver halide precipitate takes to appear.

Safety
  • Ethanol and the halogenoalkanes are highly flammable. No naked flames. Use an electric water bath, not a Bunsen burner.
  • Halogenoalkanes are harmful and their vapour is irritating. Use small amounts in a well-ventilated room or fume cupboard.
  • Silver nitrate solution (0.05 mol dm−3) stains skin and is an irritant to eyes. Wear eye protection and gloves.
