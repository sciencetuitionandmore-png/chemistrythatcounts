Catalysts for hydrogen peroxide

Core practical 3.16

Aim: Investigate the effect of different solids on the catalytic decomposition of hydrogen peroxide solution.

Safety
  • Wear eye protection. Hydrogen peroxide solution is an irritant and can bleach skin.
  • Lead(II) oxide is toxic. Do not breathe in the powder and wash your hands afterwards.
  • Manganese(IV) oxide is harmful. Use a spatula, not your fingers.
  • Do not put the bung in too tightly; the gas must be able to reach the syringe.

Test each solid with 20 cm³ of the same hydrogen peroxide solution. Use 0.50 g of each solid. Measure the volume of oxygen collected in 60 s. Also do a test with no solid (the control).

Method

  1. Choose a solid (or no solid).
  2. Measure 20 cm³ of hydrogen peroxide solution into the conical flask.
  3. Weigh 0.50 g of the solid.
  4. Push the syringe plunger in so it reads 0 cm³.
  5. Add the solid, put the bung in quickly and start the stopwatch.
  6. Read the volume of gas in the syringe after 60 s.
  7. Test the gas with a glowing splint.
