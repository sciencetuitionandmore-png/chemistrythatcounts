About Chemistry That Counts

I've taught chemistry for more than 30 years, including 18 years at an international school teaching Pearson Edexcel International GCSE and International A Level.

Everything here comes from that classroom: the practicals students find hardest to picture, the questions where marks slip away, and the step-by-step thinking that gets them back. The animations, labs and videos are free for students. The lessons and tests that go with them are in my TES shop.
