Exam tip

  • Always give the direction and the reason: "the position moves to the right, the side with fewer moles of gas, to reduce the pressure".
  • For temperature, first say whether the forward reaction is exothermic or endothermic. Raising the temperature moves the position in the endothermic direction.
  • A catalyst does not change the position of equilibrium. It speeds up the forward and backward reactions equally, so equilibrium is reached faster.
  • At equilibrium the concentrations are constant, not equal. Do not write "the reaction has stopped".

Key fact: if a system at equilibrium is changed, the position of equilibrium moves to oppose the change; only a change in temperature changes the value of the equilibrium constant.
