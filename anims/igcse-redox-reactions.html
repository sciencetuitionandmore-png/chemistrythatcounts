        Key points

        • Oxidation is gain of oxygen. Reduction is loss of oxygen.
        • Oxidation is loss of electrons. Reduction is gain of electrons. Remember OIL RIG.
        • Oxidation and reduction always happen together. A reaction with both is a redox reaction.
        • The reducing agent is the substance that is oxidised. It takes oxygen away, or it gives electrons.
        • The oxidising agent is the substance that is reduced. It gives oxygen, or it takes electrons.
        • Examples: in a displacement reaction the more reactive metal is oxidised. In rusting, iron is oxidised by oxygen (water must also be present).
