    Exam tip

    • A chiral carbon has four different groups attached. Check each group all the way along the chain: CH2CH3 and CH3 are different.
    • Draw the two enantiomers as 3D mirror images, using wedge and dash bonds and a mirror line.
    • For a racemic product, say: the carbocation (or C=O) is planar, so the nucleophile can attack from either side with equal probability, giving a 50:50 mixture.
    • Say that a racemic mixture is optically inactive because the two enantiomers rotate plane-polarised light equally in opposite directions, so the effects cancel.
