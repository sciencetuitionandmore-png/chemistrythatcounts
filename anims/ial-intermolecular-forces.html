Exam tip

  • Boiling a molecular substance overcomes the intermolecular forces. Covalent bonds inside the molecules are not broken.
  • For a hydrogen bond diagram show δ+ on H, δ− on O/N/F, a lone pair on the O/N/F, and the hydrogen bond as a dashed line from H to that lone pair, in a straight line (180°).
  • London forces: describe an instantaneous dipole that induces a dipole in a neighbouring molecule. More electrons → stronger London forces.
  • When comparing two substances, name the strongest force in each, then say which needs more energy to overcome.

Exam practiceExam-style questions

Pearson Edexcel IAL style. 11 marks. Answer each question, then check the multiple choice and use the mark scheme to mark your written answers.

Key fact: the more energy needed to overcome the forces between molecules, the higher the boiling temperature: hydrogen bonds > permanent dipole–dipole > London forces (for molecules with similar numbers of electrons).
