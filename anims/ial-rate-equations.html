    Exam tip

    • Orders come from experimental data, never from the numbers in the balanced equation.
    • For units of k, rearrange the rate equation, write in the units and cancel. Overall order 2 gives dm3 mol−1 s−1.
    • A species that is zero order does not take part in the rate-determining step (or any step before it).
    • A constant half-life on a concentration–time graph means first order. Show at least two half-lives on the graph.
