    Exam tip

    • Name an ester alcohol part first (methyl, ethyl…) then acid part (…anoate). CH3COOCH2CH3 is ethyl ethanoate.
    • Give conditions in full: esterification needs concentrated H2SO4 and heat; acid hydrolysis needs dilute acid and heat under reflux.
    • Alkaline hydrolysis goes to completion and gives the carboxylate salt, not the acid.
    • The observation for acyl chlorides with water, alcohols or ammonia is steamy (misty) fumes of HCl. Do not just write "gas".
