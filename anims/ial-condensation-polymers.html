    Exam tip

    • Draw the repeat unit in brackets with n, and make the end bonds go through the brackets.
    • Name the small molecule lost: H2O from a carboxylic acid, HCl from an acyl chloride.
    • A repeat unit from two monomers contains one of each, with no –OH, –H or –Cl ends left on it.
    • Acid hydrolysis of a polyamide gives –NH3+ (an ammonium salt); alkaline hydrolysis gives –COO− (a carboxylate salt).
