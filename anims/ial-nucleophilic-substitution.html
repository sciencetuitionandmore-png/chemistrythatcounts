Exam tip

  • The first curly arrow must start at a lone pair on the O of OH− (not at the minus sign) and end at the δ+ carbon. The second starts at the C–X bond and ends at X.
  • SN2 transition state: square brackets, dashed partial bonds to O and Br, and the negative charge outside the brackets.
  • SN1: show the C–Br bond breaking first to give a carbocation with the + on the carbon, then OH− attacking it.
  • Rate depends on bond enthalpy, not polarity: C–I is the weakest bond, so iodoalkanes react fastest, even though C–Cl is the most polar.
