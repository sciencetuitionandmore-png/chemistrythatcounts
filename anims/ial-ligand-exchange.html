    Exam tip

    • Show a dative bond as an arrow from the ligand's lone pair to the metal ion, or say both electrons come from the ligand.
    • With NH3(aq), give both observations: a pale blue precipitate first, then a deep blue solution in excess.
    • Explain the change from 6 to 4 ligands with Cl− by size: Cl− is larger than H2O.
    • For the chelate effect, count the particles on each side and say entropy increases. ΔH is about zero.
