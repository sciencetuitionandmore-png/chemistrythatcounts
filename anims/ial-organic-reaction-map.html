Exam tip

  • Distil or reflux? Primary alcohol → aldehyde: distil it off as it forms. Primary alcohol → carboxylic acid, or secondary alcohol → ketone: heat under reflux.
  • Always name the solvent: KOH or NaOH in water gives substitution (alcohol); KOH in ethanol gives elimination (alkene). KCN and NH3 are used in ethanol.
  • LiAlH4 and Grignard reagents must be in dry ether; add dilute acid afterwards. Say “concentrated” for H2SO4 in esterification and nitration.
  • In a multi-step synthesis, give reagents and conditions for every step and name each intermediate. Count the carbons: KCN and Grignard + CO2 add one carbon.
