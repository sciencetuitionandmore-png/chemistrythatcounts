    Exam tip

    • Colour comes from a d electron absorbing light and moving to a higher d orbital. Do not say the electron "gives out" the colour.
    • Say we see the complementary colour, because the light that is not absorbed is transmitted or reflected.
    • Explain colourless Sc3+ and Zn2+ using 3d0 and 3d10: no d–d transitions are possible.
    • Remove the 4s electrons first: Fe2+ is [Ar] 3d6, not [Ar] 3d4 4s2.
