    Exam tip

    • An indicator works only if its whole colour-change range lies inside the vertical section of the pH curve.
    • At half-equivalence [HA] = [A−], so pH = pKa. Read it from the curve at half the equivalence volume.
    • Explain buffer action with equations: H+ + CH3COO− → CH3COOH and OH− + CH3COOH → CH3COO− + H2O. Say there is a large reservoir of both.
    • The pH at equivalence is not always 7: above 7 for weak acid/strong base, below 7 for strong acid/weak base.
