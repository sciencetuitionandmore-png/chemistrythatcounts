Part 3Check it

  1. Which of these molecules is polar?

    NH3 is pyramidal (1 lone pair on N), so the three N–H dipoles add to a net dipole. The others are symmetrical.

  2. Carbon dioxide is non-polar because .

    O is more electronegative than C, so each C=O bond is polar. The molecule is linear and symmetrical, so the equal and opposite dipoles cancel.

  3. CHCl3 is polar but CCl4 is not, because in CHCl3 .

    Both are tetrahedral. In CHCl3 the C–H dipole is much smaller than a C–Cl dipole, so there is a net dipole.

  4. A thin stream of which liquid is deflected by a charged rod?

    Only a liquid made of polar molecules is deflected. CHCl3 is polar; the other three are non-polar.

Exam tip

  • For CO2, BF3 or CCl4, say the dipoles cancel because the molecule is symmetrical. Do not say there are no polar bonds.
  • A polar molecule needs two things: polar bonds and a shape in which the bond dipoles do not cancel. Name the shape in your answer.
  • The more electronegative atom is δ−. The dipole arrow points towards the δ− atom.
  • In the charged-rod test, a polar stream bends towards the rod whether the rod is + or −. The molecules turn so their oppositely charged end faces the rod.
