Exam tip

  • The definition needs one mole of gaseous atoms forming one mole of gaseous 1+ ions. Always write state symbols (g) in the equation.
  • For Mg → Al, say the outer electron is in a 3p sub-shell of higher energy and is shielded by the 3s electrons.
  • For P → S, explain repulsion between paired electrons in a 3p orbital. Do not just say "half-filled sub-shells are stable".
  • For successive IEs, the big jump shows that an electron is removed from a new shell closer to the nucleus.
