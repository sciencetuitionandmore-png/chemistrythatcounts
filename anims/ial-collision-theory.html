Exam tip

  • Higher-temperature curve: the peak is lower and moves to the right. It starts at the origin, never touches the energy axis, and the area under it stays the same (same number of particles).
  • For temperature, write "more particles have energy ≥ Ea, so a greater proportion of collisions are successful". "More collisions" on its own does not score.
  • A catalyst does not change the curve. Draw the new, lower Ea line to the left of the old one, and say it gives an alternative route.
  • Use the phrase "frequency of successful collisions". Higher concentration or pressure increases collision frequency, not the fraction of particles with E ≥ Ea.
