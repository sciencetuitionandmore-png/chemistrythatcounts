    Exam tip

    • Always write E°cell = E°(right) − E°(left) and give the sign and unit, e.g. +1.10 V.
    • Electrons flow through the wire; ions flow through the salt bridge. Saying "electrons flow through the salt bridge" loses the mark.
    • Learn all three SHE conditions: 100 kPa H2, 1.00 mol dm−3 H+, 298 K, with a platinum electrode.
    • If asked why a feasible reaction does not happen, say high activation energy or non-standard conditions.
