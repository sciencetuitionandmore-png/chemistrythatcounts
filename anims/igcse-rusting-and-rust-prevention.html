Key points

  • Iron rusts only when both oxygen (from air) and water are present. Salt water makes rusting faster.
  • Rust is hydrated iron(III) oxide: iron + oxygen + water → hydrated iron(III) oxide.
  • Barrier methods (paint, oil or grease, plastic coating) stop oxygen and water reaching the iron. If the barrier is scratched, the iron rusts.
  • Galvanising is coating iron or steel with zinc. Zinc is more reactive than iron, so even when scratched the zinc is oxidised instead of the iron. This is sacrificial protection. Magnesium blocks on ships work in the same way.
  • Oxidation is gain of oxygen or loss of electrons. Reduction is loss of oxygen or gain of electrons (OIL RIG). Redox means both happen at the same time.
  • In rusting, iron is oxidised (Fe → Fe3+ + 3e−) and oxygen is reduced.
