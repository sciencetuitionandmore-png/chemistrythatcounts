    Exam tip

    • In the mechanism the curly arrow starts from the lone pair on the carbon of CN− (not the N, not the minus sign) and goes to the δ+ carbon.
    • Show the intermediate with a negative charge and lone pairs on the O, then protonation by H+ or HCN.
    • 2,4-DNPH shows a C=O group (aldehyde or ketone). Tollens' and Fehling's tell them apart: only aldehydes are oxidised.
    • LiAlH4 is used in dry ether. Write the reduction with [H]: CH3COCH3 + 2[H] → CH3CH(OH)CH3.
